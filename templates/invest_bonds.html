Bond basics

💡 What is a bond?

A bond is fixed-income debt — you lend money to the issuer (government or company) and receive regular coupon (interest) payments plus your principal back at maturity.

📖 Key terms

Coupon — yearly interest rate
Maturity — repayment date
Credit rating — issuer safety grade
Seniority — repayment priority if things go wrong

🌱 Why hold bonds?

Steady income stream, lower volatility than stocks, and diversification — bonds often hold up when equities fall.

⚠️ Watch outs

Credit risk (issuer default), interest-rate risk (prices fall when rates rise), liquidity (can you sell easily?) and post-tax return.
