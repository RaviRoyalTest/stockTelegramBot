📊 Understanding the Gold-Silver Ratio

What it means: The gold-silver ratio represents how many ounces of silver it takes to purchase one ounce of gold.

How to use it: When the ratio is high, silver is relatively cheap compared to gold. When low, gold is relatively cheap compared to silver.

Investment strategy: Investors can use this ratio to switch between gold and silver holdings, buying the relatively undervalued metal and selling the overvalued one.

Important note: This ratio is just one tool among many. Always consider broader market conditions, your investment goals, and diversification strategy.
