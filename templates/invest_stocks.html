Stock Profit Percentage Calculator

Calculate profit percentage for your stock investments. Add multiple stock groups to track different purchases.

💡 How it works

Profit Percentage = (Total Profit ÷ Total Invested) × 100

Annualized Return = [(1 + Total Return)^(1/Years) − 1] × 100 (shown when age is entered)

Example: If you bought 100 stocks at ₹250 each (₹25,000 invested) and made a profit of ₹5,000, your profit percentage is 20%. Tip: Add multiple stock groups and enter the holding period to see annualized returns and overall XIRR.

📉 Stock Loss Recovery Table

If a stock drops by X%, how much does it need to gain (from the new lower price) to get back to the original value?

💡 The Math Behind It

If a stock falls by d%, the new price is (1 − d/100) of the original. To reach the original price again, the gain needed is:

Recovery % = [1 ÷ (1 − d/100) − 1] × 100

A 50% drop needs a 100% gain; a 90% drop needs a massive 900% gain!
